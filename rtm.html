How Cowboy Systems runs RTM

1
Enroll & consent.

Flag a patient for RTM as part of their therapy plan of care, with consent captured in the chart.

2
Automated daily data capture.

The patient app and the AI agent send check-in questions (pain, adherence, symptoms). Each response counts toward the "days of data" in the 30-day window — the automated follow-up is the engine.

3
RTM worklist for the clinician.

Every enrolled patient's day-count, minutes logged, and which CPT codes are billable right now, in one place.

4
Time log + the required review.

Management minutes are logged for the monthly review, including the required live, interactive call.

5
Claims auto-draft at thresholds.

When day-count and time thresholds are met, Cowboy Systems drafts the claim — the therapist reviews and submits.

Questions

Does Cowboy Systems support RTM billing?

Yes — RTM is built in. Patients are enrolled with a consent flag, the patient app and AI agent capture daily data and count the days of data, a clinician worklist shows which codes are billable, and claims auto-draft when thresholds are met.

How much can a clinic earn with RTM?

Up to about $168.68 per patient per month under 2026 Medicare rates. At 20 enrolled patients that's roughly $3,400/month in recurring revenue. Actual amounts depend on payer, documentation and time logged.

Is RTM the same as RPM?

No. RPM (Remote Physiological Monitoring) covers physiological data like blood pressure. RTM (Remote Therapeutic Monitoring) covers therapy data — pain, adherence and musculoskeletal/respiratory status — which is exactly what a PT or OT practice already tracks, and what Cowboy Systems automates.
