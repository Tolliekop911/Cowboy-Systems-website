Remote Therapeutic Monitoring

Turn patient follow-up
into recurring revenue.

Cowboy Systems has Remote Therapeutic Monitoring (RTM) built in. The automated check-ins your patients already get become billable days of data, and Cowboy Systems tracks the codes, logs the time, and drafts the claims. Under 2026 Medicare rates that is up to about $168.68 per patient per month.

Book a Demo See pricing

Most clinics leave this money on the table because tracking the days of data, the minutes, and the codes by hand is a part-time job nobody has time for. Cowboy Systems already runs the patient follow-up, so RTM is not a new workflow bolted on, it is revenue falling out of the one you already have. Enroll a patient, and the system watches the thresholds and drafts the claim the moment it is billable.

01

Enroll and consent in the chart.

Flag a patient for RTM as part of their therapy plan of care, with consent captured right in the record. No separate enrollment system.

02

The follow-up you already run becomes the data.

The patient app and the AI agent send daily check-ins, pain, adherence, symptoms, and every response counts toward the days of data in the 30-day window. The automated follow-up is the engine.

03

An RTM worklist that tells you what is billable.

Every enrolled patient's day-count, minutes logged, and which CPT codes are ready to bill right now, all in one place. No spreadsheets, no guessing.

04

The time log and the required review, handled.

Management minutes are logged for the monthly review, including the required live, interactive call, so the documentation is there if anyone ever asks.

05

Claims auto-draft the moment thresholds are met.

When the day-count and time thresholds are hit, Cowboy Systems drafts the claim through the same billing engine that sends the rest of your charges. The therapist reviews and submits.
