Cowboy Systems vs Prompt

Cowboy Systems is an all-in-one, AI-native EHR and practice-management platform for physical therapy and allied health clinics. Providers complete a full initial evaluation in about 6 minutes, and claims go out clean the first time. Here is how Cowboy Systems compares with Prompt.

What Prompt is

Prompt is a modern physical-therapy EHR known for automation and a clean interface, covering documentation, scheduling and billing for rehab clinics.

What Cowboy Systems does differently

Both are modern platforms. Cowboy Systems stands apart on three things: a 24/7 AI phone receptionist that answers calls and books patients, native iPhone and Android apps for both patients and providers, and AI documentation that drafts the note so a full initial evaluation takes about 6 minutes, with the therapist reviewing and signing. Cowboy Systems is also all-in-one, adding marketing, patient reactivation and a website builder in the same login.

Questions

Is Cowboy Systems a good Prompt alternative?

Yes. Cowboy Systems is an AI-native alternative to Prompt: it adds a 24/7 AI phone receptionist that books patients, native iPhone and Android apps for patients and providers, AI documentation that completes a full initial evaluation in about 6 minutes, and built-in billing, marketing and a website builder, all in one login.

Can I move my data from Prompt to Cowboy Systems?

Yes. Onboarding includes a guided migration that reads exports from common PT systems, brings patients, appointments and documents across, checks every row before saving, and lets you undo any import in one click.

How much does Cowboy Systems cost?

Pricing is public: $249 a month for up to 2 providers, $449 a month for up to 5 with unlimited SI notes, and custom Enterprise pricing. Optional full-service billing is 4% of collections on top of the plan.
