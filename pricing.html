How much does Cowboy cost?

Starter is $249 a month for up to 2 providers. Growth is $499 a month for up to 5, with unlimited AI notes. Enterprise is custom. Every price is on this page, no sales call needed to see it.

Can I bring my patients from my current system?

Yes. The Migration Center reads exports from WebPT, Jane, Prompt, Raintree, Net Health, SimplePractice, TheraOffice, Practice Perfect, SPRY and spreadsheets. It brings patients, appointments and documents across, checks every row before anything is saved, and lets you undo any import in one click.

Is there a contract?

Plans start with a 3-month minimum, billed monthly, or you can save 15% on a 12-month term.

What does the billing service cost?

It is optional: 4% of collections, on top of your plan, with a 6-month minimum. We run eligibility, coding review, claim scrubbing, submission, ERA posting, denials and appeals.

Is the 4% instead of a monthly fee?

No. The software is always a monthly plan: $249 a month for up to 2 providers, $499 a month for up to 5, or custom for Enterprise. The 4% of collections is only for the optional full-service billing, and it is charged on top of your plan.

Does the AI write the note for my therapist?

It drafts it from the visit and the chart. Your therapist reviews it, changes what they want, and signs. Nothing is final until they do.

How long does a note take?

Timed in our own clinic: about six minutes for a full initial evaluation, about five for a re-evaluation, and 3 minutes 38 seconds for a progress note.

Is it HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Every paid plan includes a signed BAA, patient data is encrypted, and every access is recorded in an audit log that cannot be edited.

Can my therapists work from their phones?

Yes. The provider app opens the same note forms as the web, so therapists can write and sign notes, book visits, text patients and block time off.
