Websites

A customized clinic site with an integrated booking widget, wired straight into your calendar, your forms and your front desk.

Your website is your front door, and for most clinics it is a brochure that does nothing. It looks fine, lists your hours, and then just sits there while prospective patients click away to book somewhere easier. A website should convert. Cowboy Systems Websites gives you a polished, custom site with booking built in, so the visitor who lands at 10pm becomes an appointment on tomorrow's schedule instead of a bounce.

01

A site that reflects your clinic, not a template farm.

Customized to your brand, your services, your voice. Professional, fast and mobile-first, because most patients are finding you on their phone.

02

Booking built into the page.

The integrated widget lets visitors book a real appointment right on your site, no phone call, no form that lands in a void. It checks your live availability and writes the booking straight into your calendar. The moment they decide, they are in the schedule.

03

Capture every lead.

Contact and intake forms flow directly into your unified inbox and lead pipeline, so nothing gets lost and your team follows up fast. A website visitor stops being anonymous traffic and becomes a tracked, worked lead.

04

One system, end to end.

Because the site, the widget, the calendar, the forms and the follow-up all live in the same platform, there is nothing to integrate and nothing to fall through the cracks. The patient's journey from “found you on Google” to “sitting in your waiting room” is one unbroken line.
