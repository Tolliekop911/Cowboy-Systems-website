Mobile apps

Your clinic
in everyone's pocket.

A patient app that keeps people engaged between visits, and a provider app that lets your team work from anywhere, both native, both beautiful, both yours.

Book a Demo See pricing

Care does not stop when the patient walks out the door, but for most clinics, engagement does. The exercises get forgotten. The questions pile up until the next visit. The provider is chained to a desktop. Cowboy Systems ships two native apps, one for patients, one for your team, so the relationship continues between appointments and your clinicians are never tied to a workstation again.

01

The patient app: recovery in their hand.

Patients see the exact home exercise program you prescribed, with sets, reps and video demos, and log each one as they go. They track their own progress, from range of motion to pain to attendance. They view upcoming appointments, join telehealth with one tap, and message their care team when a question comes up. And they can read every message in their own language. Engaged patients get better outcomes and stick around, this is how you keep them engaged.

02

The provider app: your whole chart, anywhere.

Your team opens today's schedule, taps a patient, and has the full chart in their hand, notes, history, documents, billing, authorizations. They write notes through the same structured forms, check patients in, review billing and ask the SI assistant a clinical or coding question. Multi-clinic? Switch practices in a tap.

03

Call patients without giving out your cell.

Tap to call a patient and it rings from the clinic's number, not the provider's personal phone, so a returned call goes to the front desk, and your team's privacy stays intact.

04

Marketing on the go.

The provider app even carries the marketing dashboard and the Content Studio designer, so an owner can check the numbers or post content from the parking lot.

Why it wins

Most EHRs give you a clunky mobile web page. This is two real App Store and Play Store apps, built from the same system, in your brand.
