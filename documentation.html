Documentation

Finish your notes
before you leave the room.

Evaluations, SOAPs, progress reports, discharges and home exercise programs, with an SI assistant that drafts, proofreads and codes right alongside you.

Book a Demo See pricing

You did not spend years in school to type until 8pm. But that is the deal most EHRs give you: clunky forms, endless clicking and notes you finish at home after the kids are asleep. Worse, rushed documentation is exactly what gets claims denied and audits lost. Cowboy Systems Documentation flips it. Charting becomes fast, structured and genuinely assisted, so you are done when the visit is done, and every note holds up.

01

Notes built for therapy, not bolted on.

Evaluation, daily/SOAP, progress, re-eval, discharge, each one structured for how PTs actually think and what payers actually require. Problem lists, measurements, goals, interventions, all in the right place. No generic medical template you have to wrestle into shape.

02

Meet SI, your documentation co-pilot.

SI drafts your assessment straight from the chart's own findings, the objective data you already entered, then proofreads it for clarity and grammar. You read it, tweak it and sign. You are always the clinician of record; SI just does the typing you hate. It will also write a pre-visit brief so you walk in already knowing the story.

03

Code it right the first time.

CPT and ICD suggestions pulled from the visit. Medicare KX modifiers and therapy-threshold tracking built in. The documentation and the billing finally speak the same language, so you bill exactly what you did, and can defend it.

04

Home exercise programs patients actually do.

Build an HEP with images and video, print it or send it to the patient's phone, and, because half your patients speak a different language at home, print it in their language, instantly. Adherence goes up when the instructions are clear and in their own words.

05

One chart, zero re-entry.

The note is tied to the schedule, the claim, the outcomes and the plan of care. Write it once; it flows everywhere. Nothing gets typed twice.

Why it wins

Other EHRs make documentation the tax on your day. This one hands you your evenings back and makes your notes stronger at the same time.
