Clinics are great at treating patients and terrible at getting more of them, not because they do not care, but because marketing means stitching together five tools, an agency and a prayer. Meanwhile your best growth lever is sitting right there: the patients you already have. Cowboy Systems Marketing turns your own patient data into a growth machine, and gives you the tools to look like a brand ten times your size.

01

Win back the patients you already earned.

Automated recalls reach the people who dropped off, the discharges who could use a check-in, and the no-shows from last week, the same day. These are patients who already trust you. Reactivating them is the cheapest growth there is, and it happens on autopilot.

02

Reviews that build themselves.

Happy patients get nudged to leave a review at exactly the right moment. More 5-star reviews, higher in local search, more new patients walking in, a flywheel that spins without you touching it.

03

Campaigns and automations, no agency required.

Build audiences from your real patient data, then send email and SMS campaigns that actually land. Set up automations once, new-patient welcome, post-discharge check-in, birthday, win-back, and let them run forever.

04

One inbox for everything.

Texts, emails, portal messages, website form fills and reviews, all in a single threaded inbox, per person. No more missing a lead because it came in on the “other” channel.

05

A Content Studio in your pocket.

A Canva-style designer built right in: templates, text, photos, shapes. Design a post on your phone, drop it into the calendar and publish. Your front desk can make studio-quality content without a designer or a subscription to one more tool.

06

Know what's working.

A clinic pulse dashboard and an owner report show new patients, visits, collections, money in flight and leads, so you finally see, in dollars, what your marketing is doing.

Why it wins

You were paying seven vendors and an agency to do half of this. Now it is one login, one bill, and it is wired to the same patient data that runs your practice.
