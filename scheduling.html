Scheduling

Your entire practice,
running on one calendar.

Every appointment, every provider, every location, every class, in a single live view that doesn't just hold your schedule, it fills it.

Book a Demo See pricing

Most clinics don't have a scheduling problem. They have a seven-places-at-once problem. A paper book at the front desk. A phone ringing off the hook. A waitlist living in someone's memory. Sticky notes. A spreadsheet. The result is the same everywhere: gaps that never get filled, no-shows that quietly drain thousands a month, and a front desk that is always one phone call behind. Scheduling by Cowboy Systems ends that. One calendar becomes the single source of truth for the whole business, and it works for you, not just as a place to write things down.

01

One calendar, zero chaos.

See every provider, room and location at a glance. Drag an appointment to move it. Drop it to reschedule. Color-coded by provider and visit type so a six-hour day reads in six seconds. No more double-bookings, no more “who is in room 2,” no more flipping between tools. The whole operation, one screen.

02

A schedule that fills itself.

This is where it stops being a calendar and starts being a growth engine. Smart reminders go out by text and email automatically, and they crush no-show rates. A self-serve booking page lets patients book themselves at 11pm without tying up a single staff member. And the waitlist is automated, the moment someone cancels, the slot is instantly offered to the next person in line, so the gap closes before you even notice it opened.

03

Stop losing money to empty slots.

No-show tracking flags your repeat offenders and feeds them straight into recall and re-engagement campaigns. Every empty 45-minute block is revenue you cannot get back, so we make sure there are as few of them as humanly possible.

04

Built for the way clinics actually run.

Multi-provider. Multi-location. Classes and group sessions. Telehealth visits with one tap. Insurance and cash patients side by side. It flexes to your practice instead of forcing your practice to flex to it.

Why it wins

Other scheduling tools are glorified notebooks. This one books patients, confirms them, fills cancellations and tells you who is slipping, on autopilot.
