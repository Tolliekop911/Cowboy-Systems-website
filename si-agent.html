SI agent

The front-desk teammate
that never sleeps.

An SI voice agent that answers the phone, books appointments, confirms visits and chases down no-shows, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Book a Demo See pricing

Every missed call is a missed patient, and they are already dialing the clinic down the street. Every no-show is a slot you cannot get back. But you cannot put a human on the phone around the clock, and voicemail is where new patients go to disappear. The SI Agent answers every call in a natural voice, books patients straight into your calendar, and spends its nights doing the follow-up your team never has time for.

01

It answers. Really answers.

Not a phone tree. Not “press 1.” A natural-sounding agent that greets callers, answers the questions they actually ask, checks your availability and books the appointment into the live calendar, while your staff focuses on the patients in front of them.

02

It fills the gaps.

Cancellation at 2pm? The SI Agent is already working the waitlist. No-show this morning? It is reaching out to rebook. It makes pre-visit calls that confirm and prep patients so they show up on time and ready, which means fewer holes in tomorrow's schedule.

03

It works the hours you can't.

After close. Weekends. Lunch rushes when every line is busy. The SI Agent is on, consistent, and never has a bad day. New patients get a real answer at 9pm instead of a voicemail, and that is the difference between a booking and a bounce.

04

Texts too, both ways.

Two-way SMS so patients can confirm, reschedule or ask a quick question by text, and it all threads back into one place your team can see.

Why it wins

Hiring another receptionist is expensive and still only covers 40 hours. This covers 168, and never calls in sick.
