Built by clinicians

Clinicians built us.
We still treat patients.

Cowboy Systems grew out of Cowboy Healthcare, our own outpatient PT and yoga clinic. We believe therapists should spend their time treating patients, not wrestling with software, so we built the system we wanted to use ourselves.

Our own therapists document, schedule and bill in it every day. When something slows them down, we feel it first. That is how an initial evaluation got down to about six minutes, and why signing it now builds the home exercise program, the flow sheet and the claim.
