How we got there: a full-time therapist seeing 12 patients a day, 5 days a week, 48 weeks a year, where 10% of visits are initial evaluations and 5% are re-evaluations. Cowboy times are what we timed in our clinic (6:00, 5:00 and 3:38). The before times are 17 minutes for an initial evaluation, the pre-AI time Raintree reports for a client (source), and our estimates of 15 minutes for a re-evaluation and 10 minutes for a progress note. That saves about 421 minutes a week. Each extra visit is counted as a 45-minute session plus its note, rounded down to whole visits, at $105.33 net revenue per visit, the rate U.S. Physical Therapy reported for the second quarter of 2025 (source). A 5-provider clinic gets about 35 hours back and room for about 40 more visits a week. Your numbers will differ with your caseload and payer mix. Building the home exercise program and flow sheet by hand is not counted.

03 Built by clinicians

Clinicians built us.
We still treat patients.

Cowboy Systems grew out of Cowboy Healthcare, our own outpatient PT and yoga clinic. We believe therapists should spend their time treating patients, not wrestling with software, so we built the system we wanted to use ourselves.

Our own therapists document, schedule and bill in it every day. When something slows them down, we feel it first. That is how an initial evaluation got down to about six minutes, and why signing it now builds the home exercise program, the flow sheet and the claim.
