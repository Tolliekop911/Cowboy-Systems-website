How we got there

A full-time therapist seeing 12 patients a day, 5 days a week, 48 weeks a year, where 10% of visits are initial evaluations and 5% are re-evaluations. Cowboy times are what we clocked end to end (6:00, 5:00 and 3:38). The before times are 17 minutes for an initial evaluation, the pre-AI time Raintree reports for a client (source), and our estimates of 15 minutes for a re-evaluation and 10 minutes for a progress note. That saves about 421 minutes a week. Each extra visit is counted as a 45-minute session plus its note, rounded down to whole visits, at $105.33 net revenue per visit, the rate U.S. Physical Therapy reported for the second quarter of 2025 (source). A 5-provider clinic gets about 35 hours back and room for about 40 more visits a week. Your numbers will differ with your caseload and payer mix. Building the home exercise program and flow sheet by hand is not counted.
